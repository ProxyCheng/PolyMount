Qt Charts — read this before distributing a build
-------------------------------------------------

PolyMount draws every chart with Qt Charts. **Qt Charts is licensed under the GPL v3 or a
commercial Qt licence** — unlike qtbase, which is LGPL v3. The two are separate licences because
Qt Charts is deliberately *not* covered by the LGPL that covers the rest of Qt.

The consequence is direct: a build of PolyMount links Qt Charts, so **distributing that build
means distributing Qt Charts**, and the GPL v3 governs that distribution unless you hold a
commercial Qt licence from The Qt Company. This is the same line PyQt6-Charts and PySide6-Addons
draw, and the same reason they too ship separately from their base packages.

If that is not the licence you want for your distribution, the options are a commercial Qt
licence, or dropping the chart pages from the build.

Nothing here is legal advice. Read the Qt licensing terms for your own distribution:
<https://www.qt.io/licensing>
